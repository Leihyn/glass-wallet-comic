# The Glass Wallet - Voiceover Script

## Production Notes

**Total runtime:** ~8-10 minutes
**Characters:**
- **NARRATOR** - Calm, authoritative, documentary style
- **ALEX** - Young adult, curious, increasingly concerned then relieved
- **MAYA** - Professional, thoughtful, initially skeptical then understanding
- **GUIDE** - Warm, helpful, explaining complex concepts simply
- **SCAMMER** - Slightly sinister, predatory
- **BOT** - Robotic, cold, mechanical
- **VILLAIN** - Dark, menacing (brief)

---

## PAGE 1 - INTRODUCTION

**NARRATOR:**
Meet Alex. Freelance developer. Solana DeFi user.

**NARRATOR:**
Not a whale. Not a criminal. Just someone trying to build wealth.

**ALEX:**
Alright, let's swap some SOL for USDC on Jupiter. Simple.

---

## PAGE 2 - THE PROBLEM BEGINS

**NARRATOR:**
Alex's World. The problem begins.

**NARRATOR:**
The moment you submit, everyone sees.

**NARRATOR:**
Broadcasting transaction... to the public mempool.

**NARRATOR:**
MEV bots are watching. Always watching.

**BOT:** *(robotic, cold)*
Target acquired. Sandwich attack initiated.

**NARRATOR:**
They see your trade before it lands. They sandwich you. You get the worse price.

---

## PAGE 3 - THE COST OF TRANSPARENCY

**NARRATOR:**
The cost of transparency.

**NARRATOR:**
MEV attacks cost Solana users millions every month.

**ALEX:** *(frustrated)*
Expected 500 USDC. Received 450. Difference: negative 50 USDC.

**ALEX:** *(confused)*
How do they know my exact balance?

**SCAMMER:** *(slightly sinister)*
Hey, I see you're holding 2,340 SOL in wallet 7xKXtg... I have an investment opportunity.

**NARRATOR:**
Your wallet is public. Anyone can look it up on Solscan.

**NARRATOR:**
One wallet address. Connected to everything. Your purchases. Your positions. Your patterns.

**NARRATOR:**
Anyone with Solscan can see your entire financial life.

**NARRATOR:**
Your landlord checks before negotiating rent. Your employer sees your side income. Scammers know exactly who to target.

**ALEX:** *(vulnerable)*
I'm not hiding anything illegal. So why do I feel so... exposed?

---

## PAGE 4 - MORE CONSEQUENCES

**NARRATOR:**
More real-world consequences.

**ALEX:** *(surprised)*
Wait, my friend pays $49 for this same service...

**NARRATOR:**
Premium tier: $99 per month. Pricing personalized based on on-chain activity.

**NARRATOR:**
Price discrimination. They checked your wallet on Solscan.

**NARRATOR:**
On-chain history follows you forever.

**ALEX:** *(realization)*
It's like carrying a billboard of my bank statements everywhere I go.

**NARRATOR:**
News headlines flash: Crypto whale robbed. DeFi user doxxed after wallet linked to identity.

**NARRATOR:**
And sometimes, the consequences are worse.

**ALEX:** *(plaintive)*
I just want to use DeFi without my whole life being on display. Is that so wrong?

---

## PAGE 5 - MAYA'S PERSPECTIVE

**NARRATOR:**
Meanwhile, on the other side of the debate...

**MAYA:** *(professional, weary)*
Another day, another privacy protocol to investigate.

**MAYA:** *(presenting)*
Total anonymity has been exploited. Ransomware payments. Stolen funds. Money laundering.

**NARRATOR:**
When we can't trace anything, bad actors thrive.

**NARRATOR:**
She's not wrong.

**NARRATOR:**
Privacy coins delisted from major exchanges. Regulatory pressure mounting. The crackdown is real.

**MAYA:** *(reflective)*
But I also know what it's like to want privacy. I don't post my salary online. I don't share my bank statements.

**MAYA:** *(questioning)*
Is wanting that for crypto really so different?

---

## PAGE 6 - THE FALSE CHOICE

**NARRATOR:**
This is the choice we've been given. Full transparency versus total anonymity.

**NARRATOR:**
Expose everything. Or look like a criminal.

**ALEX:** *(frustrated)*
I don't want to be a glass wallet.

**VILLAIN:** *(dark, menacing)*
And total darkness serves people like me just fine.

**NARRATOR:**
Neither extreme works.

**ALEX:** *(hopeful)*
There has to be another way.

**MAYA:** *(curious)*
What if privacy didn't have to mean invisibility?

**NARRATOR:**
What if you could choose what to reveal? And what to protect?

---

## PAGE 7 - DISCOVERING THE SOLUTION

**NARRATOR:**
Alex discovers something different.

**ALEX:** *(curious)*
Wait... what's this? Encrypt.trade?

**NARRATOR:**
Selective privacy. Not hiding. Choosing.

**GUIDE:** *(warm, welcoming)*
Hey there. Let me show you something different.

**GUIDE:**
It's not about hiding. It's about choosing who sees what.

**ALEX:**
I'm listening.

**GUIDE:** *(explaining)*
Right now, when you trade on Solana, it's like sending cash in a clear envelope. Everyone handling it can see what's inside.

**GUIDE:**
With encrypt.trade, your envelope is sealed. The network can verify it's valid. But they can't see what's inside.

---

## PAGE 8 - HOW IT WORKS

**NARRATOR:**
How it actually works.

**GUIDE:**
Here's what stays encrypted: Your balance - no one knows what you hold. Your route - no one can front-run your path. Your intent - no one knows what you're about to do.

**GUIDE:**
Think of it like this. Regular Solana DeFi is a glass building. Everyone sees everything.

**GUIDE:**
Encrypt.trade is frosted glass. Activity verified. Details private.

**NARRATOR:**
You can still do everything you need: Swap privately using all of Jupiter's liquidity. Bridge to Zcash with cross-chain privacy. Send SOL or any SPL token without broadcasting to the world.

---

## PAGE 9 - THE KEY INSIGHT

**NARRATOR:**
The key insight: selective privacy.

**ALEX:** *(excited realization)*
Wait. So I'm not becoming invisible. I'm just... choosing what's visible?

**GUIDE:**
Exactly.

**NARRATOR:**
Total transparency leaves you vulnerable. Total anonymity raises red flags and enables abuse. Selective privacy? That's just... normal. That's how privacy works in the real world.

**NARRATOR:**
You don't post your bank statements. But you file taxes. That's selective privacy.

**ALEX:** *(hopeful)*
So I can trade without being front-run... without everyone seeing my balance... without painting a target on myself.

**GUIDE:**
And if you ever need to prove something - for taxes, for compliance - you can choose to reveal it.

**NARRATOR:**
Swap complete. Private. Balance encrypted. Route hidden.

**ALEX:** *(relieved, happy)*
That's... that's what I wanted all along.

Selective privacy... so compliance is still possible when needed.

**MAYA:**
But users aren't exposed by default.

**MAYA:** *(warming up)*
That's actually... reasonable.

**ALEX:**
I can protect myself without looking like I have something to hide.

**NARRATOR:**
Privacy and accountability. Not opposites. A balance.

**MAYA:**
And accountability is still possible. It's opt-in, not opt-out.

**NARRATOR:**
Privacy isn't about hiding. It's about consent.

**ALEX:**

That's what encrypt.trade gives you.

## Recording Notes

### Pacing
- Allow natural pauses between speakers
- Give emotional moments room to breathe
- Speed up slightly during technical explanations
- Slow down for key taglines

### Tone Shifts
- Pages 1-4: Building tension, concern
- Page 5: Shift to professional, analytical
- Page 6: Dark, conflicted
- Pages 7-9: Hopeful, educational
- Pages 10-11: Resolution, optimism
- Page 12: Warm close

### Key Lines to Emphasize
- "I'm not hiding anything illegal. So why do I feel so... exposed?"
- "Neither extreme works."
- "Selective privacy. Not hiding. Choosing."
- "Privacy isn't about hiding. It's about consent."
- "I decide who sees what."
