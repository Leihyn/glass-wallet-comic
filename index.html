Synopsis

When every transaction you make is visible to the world, who's really watching?

The Glass Wallet follows Alex, an everyday Solana DeFi user, who discovers the hidden cost of blockchain transparency — MEV bots sandwiching their trades, scammers targeting their wallet, and services charging more because they can see exactly what Alex holds.

But this isn't a one-sided privacy manifesto.

The comic also presents Maya, a compliance professional who's seen how total anonymity enables money laundering and abuse. She's not wrong either.

Caught between two broken extremes — full exposure or total darkness — both characters arrive at the same question:

What if you could choose what to reveal?

Enter encrypt.trade: selective privacy on Solana. Not hiding. Choosing.
